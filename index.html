Biography

Rohit Sharma was born in 1987 in Nagpur, India. Growing up, he displayed early talent in cricket and rose through domestic ranks with exceptional performances. After making his mark in domestic cricket, he made his international debut for India in 2007.

Over the years, Rohit developed into one of the premier batsmen in the world, known for his elegant stroke play, particularly in limited-overs cricket. His rise included numerous records and match-winning performances that cemented his place in the Indian team.
